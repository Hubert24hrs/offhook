### Description (4000 chars max)

**The world's smartest excuse engine — powered by real AI and your real world.**

Never get caught using the same excuse twice. OFFHOOK connects to real-world data — your live weather, your actual location, the day and time — to generate hyper-believable excuses that are grounded in your reality, personalised to every contact, and impossible to repeat.

**HOW IT WORKS**

Tell OFFHOOK who you're excusing yourself to, pick a category and tone, and hit Generate. In seconds, you'll have:

• A fully crafted excuse message ready to copy or share
• A Risk Score (1–100) telling you exactly how detectable it is
• A Delivery Tip explaining how and when to say it
• A Follow-Up Suggestion for what to say an hour later to seal the deal
• Supporting context — a real-world fact that backs your excuse

**NEVER REPEAT YOURSELF**

OFFHOOK remembers every excuse per contact. Our anti-repetition engine tracks used themes across the last 30 days and makes sure you never recycle. Each person you save gets their own excuse history, cooldown tracking, and relationship context.

**REAL CONTEXT. REAL BELIEVABILITY.**

OFFHOOK pulls live data before every generation:
→ Your GPS location (city, neighbourhood)
→ Live weather conditions via Open-Meteo
→ Current time and day of the week
→ Your cultural region (for localised phrasing)

Your excuses reference things actually happening around you — making them instantly verifiable and wildly credible.

**CATEGORIES**

**DELIVERY MODES**

Apologetic • Casual • Professional • Urgent • Funny

**PRO FEATURES — OFFHOOK PRO**

Upgrade to unlock the full arsenal:
• Unlimited excuses (free tier: 5/day)
• All excuse categories and tones
• Proof Generator — craft believable evidence
• Alibi Builder — full story mode with timeline
• Delivery Coach — master your delivery in real time
• Conversation Simulator — practice handling pushback
• Unlimited contacts
• Ad-free
• Voice Mode — hear your excuse read aloud
• Offline Mode — works without internet
• Priority AI — faster generation, smarter responses

**PRIVACY FIRST**

Your excuse history stays on your device. We never store or share your personal excuse data. API calls to Claude AI are stateless — we never log your prompts.
